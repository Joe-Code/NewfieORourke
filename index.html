About the Band

Newfie O'Rourke brings together the deep Celtic roots shared by Newfoundland and Ireland, weaving jigs, reels, sea shanties, and heartfelt ballads into an evening of music that feels both ancient and alive. Rooted in the oral tradition of The Rock's outports and the pubs of County Cork, the band honours the music that crossed the Atlantic and took on a life of its own in Newfoundland.

🎻 The Sound

Fiddle, banjo, bodhrán, tin whistle, accordion, and guitar come together in rollicking sets and slow airs that'll have you on your feet — or wiping a tear.

⚓ The Tradition

Our repertoire spans centuries of Newfoundland Irish tradition: Fishermen's laments, kitchen-party stompers, and songs from the old sod preserved on the island long after they faded in Ireland.

🌊 The Story

The Irish arrived on the shores of Newfoundland as early as the 1700s, bringing their music with them. Newfie O'Rourke celebrates that unbroken thread running from Waterford to Witless Bay.

🍺 The Spirit

Every show is a session — audience participation welcome. Whether it's a festival stage, a pub session, or a community hall, Newfie O'Rourke brings the kitchen-party spirit wherever they play.
